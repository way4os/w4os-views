## Changelog

### Unreleased

- change (lists): a list with its detail reads as tabs in any theme: transparent, the selected row tinted (--w4os-accent), the detail joined
- fix (detail): a place without a picture shows none, never an empty frame
- fix (lists): a list with its detail may leave the pictures out of its rows (rowPictures); the picture of a detail 280 px high at most
- new (detail): a place with its picture in full under its name; a bare text (its description) after the fields, without its label
- change (lists): a list with its detail as tabs: the whole row selects, read as text, a hover and the selected row marked
- fix (links): the chevron of a teleport link stays with its last word; the link still wraps inside its text
- change (detail): its teleport link on the field that names its place (`teleport`), never a second line of its name
- new (lists): the arrows move the selection of a list with its detail, as a list of tabs
- new (lists): a number with its unit (60 min, 1,024 m²); a duration in minutes or hours (unit_minutes, unit_hours, unit_hours_minutes)
- fix (lists): a text keeps its line breaks, escaped (a description of several lines)
- fix (links): one click copies on a page of http too (a selected text copied); the address shown to copy by hand only when nothing could
- change (links): the menu of a teleport link in one order (teleports, maps, the address to copy, adding the grid last), its main link marked where it stands (`prefer` no longer moves it); `only`, the actions a page keeps of them
- new (links): the main link of a teleport link the one of the action a page prefers (`prefer`: teleport for a visit from another grid), the others in the destination's order
- new (links): adding the grid to the viewer (action addgrid) in a teleport link, first, with its icon (Tabler world-plus): the login address of a grid
- new (lists): ListHtml, the neutral HTML of a list (its sorts and pages keeping the whole query, its numbers with their decimals, the values a narrow list hides under the name, a text of no rows only when asked) and of an item (its teleport link, its fields, a small table of records); ListDetailView, a list with the detail of the item it selects beside it (below when narrow), its style and script (ListDetailView::ASSETS)
- new (links): W4OSTeleportLinks.setup(), the teleport links a page adds after its load
- change (links): what a link of a teleport link does told by its action (teleport, map, copy), its format only for an engine before actions; every map of a place in the menu
- new (links): the map of a place in the menu of a teleport link (format MAP), between its teleports and the address to copy, its icon alone when it is all a client has
- change (links): the menu of a teleport link lists all its links, the main one first and marked, each with a door (Tabler door-enter); one action only is its icon alone, without a menu (the door, or the copy button)
- new (links): the style and script of a teleport link in the package (TeleportLink::ASSETS), one source for every bridge; the link inline as any link, its menu opened by a chevron joined to its text, its content markup of the caller's (a picture and a name)
- change (requirements): PHP 8.2 or later, as the engine and the helpers
- new (links): TeleportLink, a teleport link that fits where it is seen: the main link of its client, a menu of its other links and the address to copy, the links of both clients written for a page kept in a cache
- new (links): Link, a link of a site by its kind (plain, more, button, primary), its address built for the page shown (the engine's Url), its text escaped
- new (forms): a database the grid has a default for, its default kept or chosen (`fallback`, `useDefault`)
- new (lists): the texts of a list as the engine names them (`texts` of Contract::listOf(): title, menu, placeholder, empty, count)
- fix (forms): a condition on a box met by the box as a form sends it (checked, or left out)
- new (settings): the entry of a settings form in a menu and its message once saved (`menu`, `saved` of Contract::settingsOf())
- new (forms): the fields the options of the grid set, shown and never changed (FormView `shown`)
- new (forms): a database field as its parts, its password kept unless one is typed (`connection-string`)
- new (access): who may change the settings of the site, as the contract says it (`Contract::siteAccess()`, `x-site`), the engine's rule without it
- new (posts): the stream of an avatar for the avatar itself (PostsView, Contract::postsOf()): the fields that write a post as the engine describes them, the actions of its posts, the requests that do them; nobody else gets one
- new (items): the form of an item (FormView): the roles of the viewer, its groups with the fields it sees, the fields it changes with their value, the ones it may not, and what a posted form changes
- new (items): the view of an item (ItemView): its header (name and picture as its list shows them, round for a person; its state, the indicators of its row; its actions, the safe ones first) and its groups of fields without what the header shows
- new (lists): the view of a filter (FilterView): its field (one value, several, or a value written in its format), its values with their label key, text and help, the values the page asks; ListView gives its filters and counts the ones asked from them
- new: the library, its repository, its tests and its build
- new: the contract (Contract), who may do an operation (Access), the message once an action is done (Done), the dates (Dates)
- new: the view of a list (ListView): its columns, its rows (RowView) with their indicators (IndicatorView) and actions (ActionView), the rules of a row (Rows)
- new: a filter the bridge fixes to one value (the grid of the site) leaves its column and the filter out of the view
